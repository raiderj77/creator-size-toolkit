Fast export advice

  • Crop to the target aspect ratio before resizing to final pixels.
  • Keep master files larger than delivery files so you can repurpose them later.
  • Test thumbnail text and subject placement at small sizes before publishing.

Use the resize calculator on the homepage when you need quick scaled dimensions.
