Instagram Guide

Instagram image sizes that keep your posts looking clean

Use these starting dimensions when exporting portrait, square, story, and reel cover graphics.

Recommended sizes

Format Size
Portrait post1080 x 1350
Square post1080 x 1080
Story1080 x 1920
Reel cover1080 x 1920

Practical tips

  • Keep important text away from the outer edges on stories and reel covers.
  • Portrait posts often take up more screen space and can perform better for visual content.
  • Batch your exports with one master layout and platform-specific crops.
