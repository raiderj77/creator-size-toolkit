FAQ

Common questions people search for

What does aspect ratio mean?

Aspect ratio describes the relationship between width and height, like 16:9 or 4:5. It helps you keep designs proportional across platforms.

Should I resize or crop first?

Usually crop first to the target shape, then resize to the final export dimensions. That keeps the composition cleaner.

Can this replace Photoshop?

No. This site is best for planning sizes and proportions quickly before you export in your main editor.
